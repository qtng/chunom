# PA script (side project)

`index.html` is the page https://chunom.org/chunom/pa/ (not in the menu): a one-page guide to the PA script for learners of Hán Nôm ("PA in one hour"), all glyphs in the font PA Pen (`js/fonts.js` of qatt.org). Sections: the idea, a syllable built part by part (examples cá, các, bản, nhiều, thuyền; stacked or side by side), the charts of the initials (each with its Hán character and the reading that gives the sound, a few Nôm readings: 𠂎 ấy, 几 ghế, 之 gì, 耒 rồi), vowels and glides, finals (the characters of the initials, -nh 儿, -u and -i the vowel strokes ô and i) and tones (5; the explicit ngang mark is left out), a field to try it, a reading of the first four lines of Truyện Kiều (tap a word for its reading) and the whole chart of stems. Every reading comes from `js/pa-text.js` and `js/parser.js` of qatt.org; the look (hero, steps, quiz) from `css/learn.css` of qatt.org (the course Learn QATT). English and Vietnamese (`data-vi` on every text).

`spec.html` is the specification (moved out of the old page): the grammar (stem = onset + nucleus, extension = coda + tone | tone), the 61 characters and their names (`PA ONSET B`, `PA VOWEL AW`, ...) in `U+F00B0..U+F00EE`, directly behind the QATT range, the own joiner and combiner (`U+F00B0`, `U+F00B1`), the stacked cell, the reading of Quốc Ngữ and the conversion from the earlier font `pa.ttf`. The PA font is a file of its own; only the code points are kept compatible with QATT, so a font for both could be built later. The old page of the archive is still at `/pages/pa/`.

PA is typed in the writer (`writer.html`, the editor of `qtng/qatt` `js/qatt-writer.js`): on this site PA replaces QATT there: the switch "Phonetic annotation" turns it on (`writer.html?pa=1` has it on from the start, which is what the guide links), then Space converts the word before the cursor to PA and Enter picks the first Nôm character (`js/pa-text.js` of `qtng/qatt` splits a syllable with `parser.js` and writes it back). The fonts are in the bucket cdn.qatt.org. The font build is in the private repository `qtng/chunom-private`.

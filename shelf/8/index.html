Summary[AI]

A rhymed prose piece (phú) on “reform” (cải lương), in the collection of poems “Quế Sơn tam nguyên thi tập”. Written in a lively, spoken style that strings together dozens of proverbs, it is the author’s call to his “brothers” to think and to work in a changing world. [AI]

He urges them to take up farming, trade and the crafts, to learn, and to give up idleness, empty show and the pursuit of rank. Old customs should be changed, but new fashions must not be followed blindly. He asks them to keep faith with friends, parents and ancestors, and to work together for the country, since all Vietnamese are of one family.

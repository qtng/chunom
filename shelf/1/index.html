Trống Quân Tân Truyện

𤿰trống軍quân新tân傳truyện

Antiphon on Literature, AB. 31

79 lines transcribed by trungDate: ca. 1946-1956

Summary AI-generated

A song in the antiphonal folk style of trống quân, with the voice of a suitor (“anh”). To a girl he offers one popular tale after another — Lý Công, Tống Trân and Cúc Hoa, the Tale of Kiều, the Butterfly and the Flower, the Twenty-Four Filial Exemplars and many more, “thirty-six tales” in all — each introduced by “or shall we hear the story of…?”. Most tales are about love and marriage, so the list is also a playful way of courting: it ends with him telling her the whole matter from first to last. The last line is cut off.

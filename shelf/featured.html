排bài歌ca⿰粦武răn棋cờ泊bạc Bài Ca Răn Cờ Bạc
A moral poem of 1921 in the song thất lục bát metre, 108 lines long, spoken by a woman to both women and men. She mocks the educated men who waste their days on cards, dice and character riddles while the world races for rank and riches. Her target is gambling, which she sees as the ruin of the household, and she portrays the gambler with sharp irony. 108 lines transcribed by phankhanhtoan2002Date: 1921

Story of Butterflies and Flowers (woodblock version) · A verse tale in dialogue between a butterfly and a flower, in the woodblock edition reprinted in January 1916 by Quán Văn Đường. In the spring the butterfly courts the flower, who answers with doubts about the sincerity and the fate of such love. 404 lines transcribed by trungDate: reprint 01/1916
胡hồ春xuân香hương詩thi集tập Hồ Xuân Hương Thi Tập
A collection of short poems attributed to Hồ Xuân Hương, in 31 pages (poems), of which the transcription is still unfinished. The subjects are everyday objects and sights – a well, a fan, a sleeping woman, the moon, caves and mountain passes – treated with the wit and double meanings for which the poet is known. 196 lines transcribed by phankhanhtoan2002Author: Hồ Xuân Hương
訓huấn女nữ演diễn歌ca Huấn Nữ Diễn Ca
A teaching poem in lục bát “for the instruction of girls” (huấn nữ diễn ca), 184 lines. It sets out how a woman should live, from her girlhood in her parents’ house through marriage and motherhood, by the ideals of the old Confucian “three obediences and four virtues”. 184 lines transcribed by phankhanhtoan2002Date: 1946-1956

Lấy Chồng Chung

𥙩lấy𫯳chồng終chung

Sharing a Husband

Hồ Xuân Hương

Summary AI-generated

A bitter poem of eight lines about being a co-wife. While one wife lies warm under the quilt, the other lies in the cold; the husband comes “twice a month, if at all”, and the effort she puts in is like work for hire without pay. She ends by thinking that, had she known, she would have stayed single.

雙song鳳phụng奇kỳ縁duyên

43 lines transcribed by trungDate: ca. 1946-1956

Summary[AI]

A verse tale of Chiêu Quân (Wang Zhaojun) in the Han dynasty. She is the only daughter of a couple from Việt Châu, beautiful and well taught; one autumn night she dreams that she meets the Han emperor, who asks about her family and name, and in the end he makes her a consort of the western palace. The transcription covers only the first part.

Mỹ Nữ Cống Hồ

美Mỹ女nữ貢cống胡hồ

Story of a beautiful woman

Summary AI-generated

A lament in the voice of a beautiful woman who is sent away as a tribute to the northern “Hồ” (barbarians). She compares herself with Tây Thi and other famous beauties, complains of flatterers at court and of the fate of women, and accepts her lot with sadness. It is transcribed in seven pages.

Summary AI-generated

A long verse tale of two poor brothers from Bình Lục, Nguyễn Đạt and Nguyễn Sinh, who leave home to study for the examinations. Sinh, burly and plain of face, is mocked by the daughter of a judge’s house, Ngọc Thanh, and ends up before the judge himself, where the servants jeer and he eats his bowl of rice unabashed; instead of driving him out the judge sets him a poem on the moon, finds a worthy man in him and keeps him in the house to study. Letters pass between the brothers, both take the prize at Sơn Nam and then pass as đồng tiến sĩ, and the judge gives Ngọc Thanh to Sinh – the tale closing on the lesson that good meets good and that the merit of the forebears brings worthy children.

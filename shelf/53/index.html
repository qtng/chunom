Summary[AI]

An excerpt of a prose biography (1926) of Phan Bội Châu, the Vietnamese reformer and opponent of colonial rule: his mother and early life, his years abroad in Guangdong and Japan, the death sentence passed in his absence, his arrest by the French in Shanghai in 1925, and the public campaign – students, women’s petitions to the Governor-General, an appeal by a French socialist – for his release.

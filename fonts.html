QATT Pen fonts

A pen line for Chữ Nôm, Han characters and Quốc Ngữ, in four weights to download.

The most common characters are built in; the full font (about 10 MB) is loaded only when you type a rare one.

One family: install all four and the font menus show them as the styles Thin, Light, Regular and Medium of "Qatt.org-Hannom Pen".

The QATT letters are © qatt.org; the strokes of the other characters are drawn from the data of GlyphWiki (KAGE, GPL-3.0). See the licence inside the font.

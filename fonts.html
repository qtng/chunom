QATT Pen fonts

Quốc Ngữ • Chữ Nôm • Quốc Âm Tân Tự

All three scripts in one modern font, in four weights, to download for your desktop.

The most common characters are built in; the full font (about 10 MB) is loaded only when you type a rare one.

QATT stands for Quốc Âm Tân Tự, a phonetic script for Vietnamese. This font has the QATT letters together with Han characters and Chữ Nôm.

One family: install all four and the font menus show them as the styles Thin, Light, Regular and Medium of "Qatt.org-Pen".

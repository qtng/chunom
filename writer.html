Chữ Nôm writer

Type Vietnamese, then pick the Nôm character that fits.

  1. Type a syllable with Telex, for example viet → việt.
  2. A list of Nôm characters appears below the text field: tap one, or press 1–0. With ↑↓ you move through the list, Esc closes it.
  3. With QATT on, a syllable you do not pick a Nôm character for stays in Quốc Ngữ until you press Space: then it turns into Quốc Âm Tân Tự. Enter picks the first Nôm character, or converts when the list is empty.

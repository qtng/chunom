Chữ Nôm Writer

Type Vietnamese with Telex, then pick the Nôm character that fits.

  1. Type a syllable with Telex, for example viet → việt.
  2. A list of Nôm characters appears below the text field: tap one, or press 1–0. With ↑↓ you move through the list, Esc closes it.
  3. With QATT on, a syllable you do not pick a Nôm character for stays in Quốc Ngữ until you press Space or Enter: then it turns into Quốc Âm Tân Tự. Space picks the first is switched off for this and Enter picks the first is off as well: pick a Nôm character with its number, with ↑↓ and Enter, or by tap.

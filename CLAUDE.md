# qtng/chunom

The dictionary site of Chữ Nôm (search, character details popup, decks and practice, Nôm writer, Génibrel and Bonet dictionaries) in `index.html`, `writer.html`, `decks/`, `dict/`, `js/`, `css/`, with the data `dict.csv`, `js/*.json`.
Served at `/chunom/` of the Chunom site. Every page links `https://qatt.org/css/site.css` right after Bootstrap, and `js/navigation.js` writes the theme file of qatt.org into the head: the theme chosen on this site (palette button next to EN | VI, stored per site; see "Themes" in `qtng/qatt` `CLAUDE.md`). The pages have the skeleton of qatt.org (a `.hero` header, `.card-box` panels, the button classes `btn-cinnabar`, `btn-quiet`, …, colours from the `--ink`/`--paper`/`--accent` variables, only layout in a page's own `<style>`), so that the themes of qatt.org work here too; a new page follows it, and every change needs a look in the themes (at least classic and the dark `future`). The pages: `translate.html`, `writer.html`, `index.html` (the search: the hero holds the links to the other parts as `btn-quiet btn-pill`), `dict/` (the viewer builds its hero in `viewer.js`: the dictionaries as buttons; the title stays small in every theme, the scan matters), `decks/` (`index.html`, `deck.html`: the hero holds the deck's cover, title and buttons; `practice.html` with `css/practice.css` and `js/practice.js`; its buttons are `btn-cinnabar`/`btn-quiet`, its boxes take `Canvas` and `--paper`; `immersive.html` stays dark in every theme, the theme only gives its fonts and the start button) (the pattern: the `.hero` header with a `.han` label in the Nôm font, a title, a lead and two `btn-cinnabar btn-pill` links to related pages; panels `.card-box`; the classic look of `.hero` in the page's own `<style>`, as on qatt.org). Colours of a page's own boxes that must work in a dark theme too: the variables (`--ink`, `--muted`, `--line`, `--field`), `color-mix(in srgb, currentColor 4%, transparent)` for a tint, `Canvas`/`CanvasText` for a popup (the dark theme sets `color-scheme: dark`). To look at a page in a theme: `?theme=<name>` in the address (it is then also stored). The menu (`js/navigation.js`, also the translation `Lang`) is the one of `qtng/qatt`, loaded from `https://qatt.org/js/navigation.js`; the logo and icons of the Chunom site are in `img/`. The glyphs of QATT (`qattCodes()` in `js/detail.js`) are `<qatt-code>` elements that `qatt.js` draws as SVG; with the option `qattFont` (e.g. `'QATT Pen'`, set by `index.html`, which then loads `qatt-text.js` and `fonts.js` of qatt.org and no `qatt.js`) they are `<span class="qatt-glyph">` with text of that font (`QattText.word`, as in the editor; no SVG, no observer). The practice (`practice.js`, decks) and `immersive.html` still draw SVG. `js/detail.js` (the details popup) is also loaded by pages of other sites, so it finds its files from its own script URL; its colours come from the variables of the page with the classic values as fallback (`--dm-*`), its colour rules start with `:where(#detailModal)` so that the rules of a theme win, its buttons are `btn-quiet` / `btn-cinnabar`.
Images, audio, fonts and scans are not in this repository but in `qtng/chunom-assets` (`/chunom-assets/…`). The landing pages for search engines are generated in `qtng/pages` from `dict.csv` and `js/glyph_relations.json`; after changing those files, regenerate there.
`js/check_font_coverage.py` reports which characters of the data a font covers.
`writer.html` has the editor of `qtng/qatt` (`js/qatt-writer.js`, loaded from `https://qatt.org/`, the same as `editor.html` there; here `QattWriter.mount(…, { switch: 'qatt' })`: Nôm candidates always on, the switch turns QATT on and off), the page keeps only the header and the info box; change the editor in `qtng/qatt`, and raise the `?v=N` of the script tag in `writer.html` (and in `editor.html` there) with every change: the file is cached for 4 hours. `translate.html` (Quốc Ngữ → Chữ Nôm) is the translator: the page builds the Chữ Nôm options of every syllable and compound (`dict.csv`, `js/ime.js`; the usual character of the dictionary is marked `*`) and sends them to the Supabase Edge Function `translate` (source in the private repository `qtng/chunom-private`, project ChunomApp). The model only chooses from the lists in the context of the sentence and may name a character of its own only where no option fits (shown as uncertain), so the reading of a chosen character is right by construction; the page rates every word (from the list / model's own / uncertain). A click on a word of the result that has other options (`has-alts`) opens a list (`#pick`) to choose another character from them (the model's next best first); the chosen character replaces it in the text and the copy, the replaced one becomes an alternative, the word is rated "chosen by you". A word without a character (and one that is not from the list) gets a dialog with a field of `js/ime.js` (Telex and Nôm candidates) to type one; the other words have the same field behind "Type another character…"; a typed character is rated "typed by you" (its reading is checked like the model's own). Only in the page, nothing is sent or stored. Words without other options, and the table, open the popup of the character as before. The request also has `context` (1 to 2, 2 if it is missing): how many words on each side of a gap of the dictionary (a word for which the model had to use a character of its own) the function stores for the improvement of the dictionary and the model (table `translate_gaps` of `qtng/qatt`, `db/translate_log.sql`, shown in its admin pages). The page sets it with the switch "Help improve the model" (on at every visit, the choice is not remembered): `CONTEXT_ON` = 2 (the maximum of the function), off `CONTEXT_OFF` = 1; one word on each side is stored in any case. A gap is stored only when the word and the 2 words on each side of it (also when fewer are stored) can be Quốc Ngữ (the syllable check `vietnamese()` of the function), so English or mangled text leaves no hints. It can also return a free translation of the sentence into a language chosen on the page (`lang`, allow-list `LANGUAGES` in the function; stored in `localStorage.translate_target`). While the answer is awaited (up to a minute: the function calls Gemini) an animation is shown: four characters in a wave, a bar that creeps forward but never reaches the end, and a message that changes with the elapsed time (`WAIT` in the page), so nobody thinks the page is broken.
The function, its prompts and the order of the model names are in the private repository `qtng/chunom-private` (`supabase/functions/translate/`, documentation in `docs/translate.md`), not here: a prompt change is a commit there and a deploy of the function (a session that has to change them needs that repository attached). The page shows the prompt version under the result.
`decks/deck.html`: the owner adds and changes items in the edit dialog ("Add item", a pencil on every row): the Chữ Nôm is typed with `js/ime.js` (Telex, candidates), the readings and the English meaning of `dict.csv` (and of `ime.js`) are suggested as chips (of the whole word, else one row per character), reading and meaning stay editable; an item of a deck is `{ n, qn, m? }`, `m` = `{ en?, de? }` (both optional, the field is revealed by a button; the list shows `en`, else `de`).
`translate_nom.html` is the way back (Chữ Nôm → Quốc Ngữ), a **draft** that is linked only from the admin area of `qtng/qatt` (`noindex`): the page cuts the text into Chữ Nôm characters, builds for every span of 1 to 4 characters the readings that `dict.csv` and `js/ime.js` (`lookupGlyph`) know and shows a reading from the dictionaries alone ("Read (dictionary only)", no sign-in); "Read with AI" sends the lists to the Edge Function `translate_nom` (source in the private repository, feature `translate`), which chooses from them in the context; a click on a word opens the other readings. Not yet in the menu, the log or the switch of `translate.html`.

## The sites and their four repositories

Two websites, hosted with GitHub Pages, built from four repositories of the GitHub account `qtng`:

| Repository | Served at | How |
|---|---|---|
| `qtng/qatt` | `https://qatt.org/` (root) | custom domain `qatt.org` set on this repo |
| `qtng/qtng.github.io` | `https://chunom.org/` | the user site; its custom domain is `chunom.org`; the other repos below hang under it as sub-paths |
| `qtng/chunom` | `https://chunom.org/chunom/` | project site of `qtng.github.io` (dictionary, decks, writer, dictionaries) |
| `qtng/pages` | `https://chunom.org/pages/` | project site, published from its folder `docs/` (landing pages of the characters) |
| `qtng/chunom-assets` | `https://chunom.org/chunom-assets/` | project site (images, audio, fonts, scans; no code) |

`chunom`, `pages` and `chunom-assets` are **one site** (the Chunom site, same origin); `qatt` is **a separate site** (another origin).
GitHub Pages allows one custom domain per repository, which is why `qatt` has its own domain and the others share `qtng.github.io`.
The Chunom site is live on `chunom.org` (the former test address `v2.chunom.org` is no longer used; see "Going live" for how it was done).

Old addresses that must keep working: `chunom.org/pages/<HEX>/`, `chunom.org/pages/grade/<N>/`, `chunom.org/pages/pa/`, `…/fonts/`, `…/links/` (same paths in `qtng/pages`),
`/pages/genibrel/`, `/pages/bonet/`, `/pages/ime/`, `/pages/imepad/`, `/pages/moses/`, `/pages/standard/` (redirect stubs in `qtng/pages`), and
`qatt.org/qatt/<path>` (now `qatt.org/<path>`), `qatt.org/chunom/…`, `qatt.org/chunom-assets/…`, `qatt.org/pages/…` (forwarded by `qtng/qatt/404.html`).

## URL rules (important when you add or change a link)

- **Inside the Chunom site** (`chunom`, `pages`, `chunom-assets`) use **root-relative** paths: `/chunom/js/detail.js`, `/chunom-assets/fonts/fonts.css`, `/pages/site.js`. Never write the domain, so the same file works on any domain of the site.
- **From the Chunom site to qatt.org** use **absolute** URLs: `https://qatt.org/vendor/bootstrap/…` (Bootstrap, Bootstrap Icons, Supabase), `https://qatt.org/js/qatt.js`, `parser.js`, `supabase.js`, `login.js`, `https://qatt.org/fonts/…`.
- **Inside qatt.org** use **root paths** without any folder prefix: `/js/navigation.js`, `/editor.html`, `/img/logo.svg` (the old prefix `/qatt/` no longer exists).
- **From qatt.org to the Chunom site** use **absolute** URLs with the domain: `https://chunom.org/chunom/js/ime.js`, `https://chunom.org/chunom-assets/fonts/fonts.css`.
- **Scripts that other pages load across sites** (`chunom/js/detail.js`, `ime.js`, `practice.js`) must not assume their own origin: they derive their location from `document.currentScript.src` (e.g. `new URL('../../', BASE)` = root of the Chunom site).
- Canonical links, `og:` tags and the sitemap need absolute URLs; `qtng/pages` writes them from `tools/generate.py --site …`.
- There is **one `js/navigation.js`** (menu bar, brand, bottom bar on phones, and the translation `Lang`), in `qtng/qatt`, served at `https://qatt.org/js/navigation.js`. The pages of the Chunom site load it from there with `<script src="https://qatt.org/js/navigation.js"></script>` early in `<head>` (a normal script, not `defer`, so that the page is translated while it is parsed). It picks brand, logo, icons, colours and the own block of the menu from the address of the page (`chunom.org` or the folders `/chunom/`, `/pages/`, `/chunom-assets/` = Chunom site, else qatt.org); the constants `CHUNOM_SITE` and `QATT_SITE` hold the absolute addresses for the links to the other site. The Chunom logo and icons are in `chunom/img/`.
- The Supabase login (decks, practice) is stored in `localStorage`, i.e. **per origin**: a user signed in on `qatt.org` is not signed in on the Chunom site.
- The site is plain HTML and JavaScript with Bootstrap 5.3, no build step. Local preview of the real URLs is not possible; to test, serve the repos under mocked hosts (Playwright `page.route` mapping `https://chunom.org/chunom/*`, `/chunom-assets/*`, `/pages/*` and `https://qatt.org/*` to the checkouts) and look for 404s and console errors.

## Translation (Vietnamese)

`js/navigation.js` defines `Lang` (also on the Chunom site). The language is `localStorage.lang` (`en` or `vi`) if somebody chose it (the switch EN | VI next to the menu button in the menu bar, also in `license.html` of qatt.org, or `?lang=vi` in the address), else the first of English and Vietnamese in the languages of the browser (`vi` only if it comes before `en`). It is not stored until it was chosen.
In `vi`, a MutationObserver gives every tag with `data-vi` the text of that attribute as its text content (the English text is kept and returns in `en`). It works while the page is parsed and for tags that JavaScript adds or changes later.

Rules when you write or change pages:

- A text that is translated sits **in a tag of its own without tags inside**: `<span data-vi="Ký hiệu">Symbols</span>`. Tags with tags inside are not translated. The English text stays in the tag and must not be empty.
- When the user says that something is called `xyz` in Vietnamese, put `data-vi="xyz"` on the tag of that text (wrap the text in a `<span>` if it has no tag of its own).
- When the English text of a tag that has `data-vi` changes, renew its translation too and tell the user which ones (translations written by Claude are drafts: say so).
- Texts are written in sentence case, not title case ("Symbol table", "Next word"); only app names such as Sound Check are capitalised.
- Scope: translate interface elements (buttons, menu entries, labels, option groups, placeholders) and headings. Long texts (paragraphs, explanations) stay English; single English words in a Vietnamese interface are fine, a completely foreign interface is not.
- In JavaScript just set the text of such a tag (or add one with `data-vi`); the observer translates it. `Lang.t("English", "Tiếng Việt")` gives a text for a string that is not in a tag; the event `langchange` (on `document`) is sent when the language is switched. Attributes: `data-vi-label`, `data-vi-title`, `data-vi-placeholder` and `data-vi-aria-label` give the attribute without the prefix its Vietnamese text (e.g. the `label` of an `<optgroup>`: `<optgroup label="Choose font" data-vi-label="Lựa chọn phông chữ">`).

## Going live on chunom.org (done)

`chunom.org` is the address of the Chunom site (done, with `v2.chunom.org` before). What was done, for reference or for a move to another domain:

1. DNS of `chunom.org` to GitHub Pages (A records `185.199.108.153` … `185.199.111.153`; `www` as CNAME to `qtng.github.io`), then the custom domain `chunom.org` in `qtng.github.io` → Settings → Pages and enforce HTTPS. Never use "Unpublish site" to switch domains (it removes the certificate).
2. In `qtng/qatt` the domain in `js/navigation.js` constant `CHUNOM_SITE`, `404.html` constant `CHUNOM_SITE`, `check.html`, `editor.html`, `type.html`, `admin/index.html` and the default `PROMPTS_URL` / allowed origins of the function `translate`.
3. In `qtng/pages` `python3 tools/generate.py --site https://chunom.org` (add `--chunom ../chunom --assets ../chunom-assets` for local checkouts) and commit `docs/`.
4. Nothing to change in `qtng/chunom` and `qtng/chunom-assets` (root-relative paths).
5. Search Console: verify `chunom.org`, submit `https://chunom.org/pages/sitemap.xml`.

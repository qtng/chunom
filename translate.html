Quốc Ngữ → Chữ Nôm with your own chatbot

Open a guided prompt in your chatbot. You add your text at the end, send it, and the chatbot walks you through candidates, selection and a final check.

How it works

  1. Choose your chatbot below. The prompt appears in the input box but is not sent yet.
  2. Click at the very end of the prompt and type or paste your Quốc Ngữ text (one or two sentences). Then send.
  3. Review the candidate table. Reply OK, or name candidates to remove or add.
  4. Reply OK again for the final check. The result is shown as Quốc Ngữ and Chữ Nôm text.

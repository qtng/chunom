17 November 2026

Nôm Notes: a new app for writing Chữ Nôm

  • Write notes and documents in Chữ Nôm, horizontally or vertically in columns, like old Asian texts.
  • Type Quốc Ngữ with Telex and pick the Chữ Nôm from the suggestions, or write QATT and PA directly.
  • Use headings, two-column passages and readings that sit beside the characters.
  • Choose a font, a text size and a paper style for every note.
  • Share a note with a link, and export or import your notes.
  • Sign in to keep your notes in sync on all your devices; the app works offline and can be installed on your phone or computer.
  • The eye button next to the plus button shows what is on your clipboard, in the font of your choice, and lets you save it as a new note. When you open the app or come back to it and the clipboard holds Chinese characters, Chữ Nôm, QATT or PA, a short hint offers to show it, once per text.

15 November 2026

Better Chữ Nôm suggestions while you type

  • You no longer have to type the marks. Type nuoc and 渃 nước appears at once; nam shows nam, năm, nằm, nấm … together.
  • d also finds đ: duong shows dương and đường.
  • Every mark you type narrows the list: nươc keeps only nước, nắm only nắm. The tone can be written anywhere in the word: hoà and hòa give the same suggestions.
  • Order: words written exactly as you typed come first, then shorter before longer, then characters you choose often or that have a meaning, then alphabetical.
  • Works wherever you type Chữ Nôm.

Stroke order animations for almost every character

8 November 2026

PA is back, better than ever

  • PA (phonetic annotation) is back on the website. It had been announced that it would be removed, but many users asked for it, so it is here again.
  • It is better than ever: the font now supports stacked characters.
  • Available wherever you can type Chữ Nôm.
